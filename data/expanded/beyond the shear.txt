# The region beyond the shear: seven systems past Suhail that no hyperspace
# route reaches. Only the player's carrier can get there, with a fold jump from
# Suhail to Threshold (see PlayerInfo.cpp). Nobody lives here any more. The
# worlds have no spaceports, but the player can land to build facilities:
# 	"expanded ruin"   worlds can host a Salvage Rig (Relic Fragments)
# 	"expanded shear"  worlds can host a Shear Harvester (Shear Glass)
# and any uninhabited world can host the usual outposts.

planet "Threshold Wreck"
	attributes uninhabited station "expanded ruin"
	landscape land/station22
	description `A broken cylinder station, half a kilometer long, tumbling slowly end over end. Something cut it open along one side a very long time ago. The edges of the cut are smooth, and the air inside has long since leaked away into the dark.`
	description `	The corridors are wide and low, and the doors are a little too short for a comfortable human. Every room has the same small symbol painted beside the door, in the same careful hand.`

planet Gate
	attributes uninhabited "expanded shear"
	landscape land/lava7-sfiera
	description `The world closest to the shear has been scarred by it. Its surface rock has fused into sheets of dark glass that ring faintly when the wind blows, and the wind always blows. Your instruments give slightly different readings every time you look at them.`

planet Vigil
	attributes uninhabited "expanded ruin"
	landscape land/desert11
	description `A dry world under a pale sun, with the ruins of a city along what used to be a coastline. The buildings are low and round, and half buried in sand. Machines still move between them at night, sweeping the streets, and stop to wait whenever a ship lands.`

planet "Vigil's Eye"
	attributes uninhabited
	landscape land/canyon11

planet "Lantern Array"
	attributes uninhabited station "expanded ruin"
	landscape land/station41
	description `A ring of collecting dishes in orbit around a gas giant, most of them shattered. The control rooms are full of screens that still light up when you walk past, showing the same star chart: this region, with the shear drawn around it in a thick, confident line.`

planet Ashfall
	attributes uninhabited mining "expanded shear"
	landscape land/lava2
	description `A hot, heavy world close to its red sun, its plains covered in ash and fused glass. There are no ruins here, only mine shafts, sealed with plugs of something that has not corroded in all the time since.`
